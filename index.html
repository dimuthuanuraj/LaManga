How to find us from the main road

520 m in total — about a 1-minute drive or a 7-minute walk from the junction.

  1. On the Galle–Matara main road (A2), find the Udupila Road junction in Mirissa, just east of the town centre.
  2. Turn inland (north) into Udupila Road, away from the beach side.
  3. Continue about 400 m up Udupila Road, passing Coco Gate and Dewmini Roti Shop.
  4. Turn right into Withanagoda Road (shown as Jayalathgama Road on some maps) and carry on about 100 m into Madina Watta.
  5. LÁ MANGÁ is on your right — look for the property sign. Free parking is on site.

Arriving by tuk-tuk or taxi? Show the driver "LÁ MANGÁ, Udupila, Mirissa" or send us a WhatsApp message and we will guide your driver in.
